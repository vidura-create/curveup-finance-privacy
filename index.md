# Privacy Policy for CurveUp Finance

**Last updated: August 4, 2026**

CurveUp ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CurveUp Finance mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

---

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Name and Email Address** – Used to identify and authenticate you within the app.
- **User IDs** – Used to manage your account and session securely.

### Financial Information
- **Expense Claims and Leave Data** – Information related to your workplace expense submissions and leave requests.

### Photos
- **Receipt Images** – Photos you voluntarily attach to expense claims. These are uploaded to our secure server and used solely for processing your claims.

### Device Information
- **Device Identifiers** – Used to send push notifications to your device.

### App Performance Data
- **Crash Logs and Diagnostics** – Used to monitor app health and fix bugs.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for:
- Authenticating users and managing accounts
- Processing leave applications and expense claims
- Sending push notifications about your requests
- Improving app performance and fixing issues

---

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. All data is stored securely on CurveUp's servers and is only accessible by your organization's administrators.

---

## Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS). We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data.

---

## Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your employment relationship with your organization is active. Data deletion is managed by your organization's administrator.

---

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (via your organization's administrator)
